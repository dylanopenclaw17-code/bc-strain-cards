Option C — Data-forward — leads with potency and value

Height ~400px
THC as visual bar
$/g on the image

For a store whose advantage is volume pricing, the two numbers that decide a purchase are potency and price per gram. This puts $/g on the image where the starburst used to be, and turns the THC range into a bar you can scan down a column without reading. Best if you want shoppers comparing value quickly.
